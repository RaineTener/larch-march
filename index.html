How to play
  • 4 teams of 3–4. One host (not me!) runs this board on the TV.
  • The team that scored last picks. Any team buzzes in by slapping the table.
  • Answer in the form of a question. Wrong answers lose the points.
  • Time Flies is an audio round. Each clue has a Spotify button and a free YouTube button.
  • To keep song titles hidden, cast just this browser tab to the TV, not your whole screen.
  • One Daily Double is hidden. Final Jeopardy: every team writes down a wager and an answer.
